The deck includes sourced competitive analysis, an office-segment pilot, five proposed metrics and the risks/rollout gates. Business-case detail and source links are in `docs/BUSINESS_CASE.md` and `docs/COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md` in the repository. Targets are hypotheses, not achieved results.
