## Implications for the proposal

**Airtel Business:** Its existing portfolio supports the case's bundle breadth. The opportunity is to simplify how an owner discovers a suitable starting point and reaches a clear quote, while retaining field expertise for exceptions. The proposal complements those products; it does not claim Airtel lacks digital sales capabilities.

**JioBusiness:** Integrated business services and self-care establish that combining products is already a market offering. The pitch therefore centres on fewer unclear decisions, explicit eligibility and visible progress, rather than claiming the first integrated bundle. Do not compare catalogue fixture prices to live Jio prices as though they were like-for-like tariffs.

**Vi ReadyForNext:** Digital-readiness assessment validates the importance of structured MSME discovery. Udyam Saathi's proposed next step connects answers to catalogue eligibility and a reviewable quote, then preserves context into document review and partner help. The cited pages do not establish that Vi lacks any of these subsequent capabilities.

**Proposed differentiation to test:** Measure whether a multilingual explanation plus rule-checked pricing helps eligible professional offices complete onboarding with fewer status calls. Test against the operator's existing journey. Collect competitor customer-experience evidence before making comparative conversion or speed claims.
